# V127b East Annex: true staff corridor renovation and source-attached lighting

The **real canonical 1992 East Annex staff corridor** at world x 56..65,
z -105..-34 retains its exact original 9 x 71m footprint. It was an
implausible open 639m² section of identical retail carpet with an empty
service ceiling. This patch builds on the same shared game/film/Builder
source, with **no copied standalone preview or published owner Builder data**.

  Their real 2m source dimensions and physical ceiling-contact bounds are
  checked in both source tests and actual desktop Chromium.
- Fix **five original fictional 1997 institutional staff plaques** to the
  existing x65 masonry or source-backed actual lintels for Training T-04,
  Break Room, Staff Lockers, old Annex Stores and Employee Exit. None
  hang over open doorways or become player colliders.
- Fit **three already locally licensed and pinned closed-door CC0 filing
  cupboards** to the existing east-side staff wall BETWEEN door apertures.
  These are actual 3D models and occupy the same physical gameplay
  collision footprint in all six chapters.
